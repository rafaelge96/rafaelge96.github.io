Delete your data — Mente Despierta

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Mente Despierta, by Rafael González Escobar, has no servers or accounts of its own: we do not keep any of your data outside your device. Here is how to delete what the app stores and what Google Play Games and Game Center store, if you use them.

1. Your progress on the device

Your name (if you entered one), your games, your streak, your levels, your stars and your settings are stored only on your phone or tablet. To delete them:

  • Android: Settings › Apps › Mente Despierta › Storage › Clear data, or uninstall the app.
  • iPhone and iPad: uninstall the app (touch and hold its icon › Remove App).

Everything is deleted immediately and nothing is kept. If you have system backups turned on (Google or iCloud), they may keep a copy as they do for your other apps; that is managed from those backups.

2. Achievements and leaderboards in Google Play Games (Android)

If you signed in to Google Play Games, Google stores your Mente Despierta achievements and scores with your player profile. You can delete them yourself:

  • Open the Google Play Games app › your profile › Settings › Delete Play Games account & data. There you can delete just the Mente Despierta data or your whole player profile.
  • You can also manage it from your Google Account, at myaccount.google.com.

This deletes the achievements you earned, your leaderboard scores and the related game data. Google handles them as the responsible party under its privacy policy, including how long they are kept.

3. Achievements and leaderboards in Game Center (iPhone and iPad)

If you use Game Center, Apple stores your achievements and scores. You can sign out in Settings › Game Center, and ask Apple to delete your data at privacy.apple.com, under its privacy policy.
